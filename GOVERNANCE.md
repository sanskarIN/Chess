# Governance

Chess-Master is currently maintainer-led. Sanskar is the initial maintainer;
accepted contributors retain copyright in their contributions.

Maintainers decide releases, repository access, roadmap priority, security
embargoes, and whether changes meet the project’s chess-correctness, privacy,
accessibility, documentation, and licensing standards. Decisions should be
explained in issues or pull requests when they affect contributors.

Small changes may be accepted through review. Large features should have a
written proposal, acceptance criteria, threat/privacy analysis, tests, and a
documented maintenance owner before implementation. Compatibility-breaking,
data-migration, protocol, economy, or legal changes require explicit maintainer
approval.

No contributor is entitled to merge access, release credentials, or a response
deadline. Maintainers may delegate areas after sustained, trustworthy work.
Release signing and hosting credentials remain least-privilege and outside git.

If the project becomes inactive, GPL-3.0-or-later permits continued forks.
Trademarks, account credentials, and third-party service access are not granted
by the source license.
