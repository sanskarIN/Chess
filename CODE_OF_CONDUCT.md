# Code of conduct

Chess-Master contributors must make participation safe, respectful, and useful.

## Expected behavior

- Be patient, specific, and constructive.
- Welcome people across experience levels, languages, regions, identities, and
  accessibility needs.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Respect privacy; do not publish personal information, private reports,
  credentials, or identifying logs.
- Give credit and preserve authorship, license, and source notices.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope while remaining free to fork under
  the project license.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, stalking, doxxing,
spam, impersonation, deliberate disruption, license violations, and retaliation
are not accepted in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For conduct concerns, email `sanskarin@outlook.in`. Do not use that address to
publish another person’s sensitive information; share only what is needed to
investigate. Maintainers may edit or remove content, warn participants,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participation,
proportionate to behavior and safety needs. Conflicts involving a maintainer
should be documented and reviewed by another trusted contributor when one is
available.

This policy applies to repositories, reviews, issues, discussions, relay project
spaces, and public representation of Chess-Master.
